Lootgrind RPG — Support

Questions, bug reports and account help.

Contact

Email lootgrindrpg@gmail.com

Purchases are billed by Apple. Billing questions and refunds go through reportaproblem.apple.com, not through us.

Common questions

I deleted the app. Is my character gone?

Probably not. On iPhone the game keeps a copy of your save in the device keychain, which survives deleting and re-downloading the app. When you reinstall, it checks that copy on the first launch and restores your character automatically. The copy is kept up to date as you play, and written immediately when you leave the app or finish a purchase.

If you land on character creation instead, close the app fully and open it once more before creating anything — creating a character and playing will write a new save over the backup. Please note there is no cloud save: the game has no accounts or servers, so if the copy on your device is gone we have no way to restore your character.

I bought gems and they did not arrive

Close the app fully and reopen it while you have a connection. If the payment went through but the gems were never delivered, the App Store redelivers that purchase on the next launch and the game grants the gems then. This is the fix in almost every case.

Gem packs are consumable purchases, so Restore Purchases cannot bring them back — that button is for Remove Ads. Your gems live in your save on your device, and the game has no accounts or servers, so we cannot add gems to your account remotely.

Purchases are billed by Apple, and billing questions and refunds are handled by Apple rather than by us. To request one, go to reportaproblem.apple.com and sign in with your Apple Account.

What does Restore Purchases do?

It restores the one-time Remove Ads purchase after a re-download, or on another device signed in to the same Apple Account. The game also checks for it automatically each time it starts. Gem packs are consumables and cannot be restored this way.

I bought Remove Ads but still see ads

Remove Ads stops the forced ads. The optional reward ads stay available on purpose, so you can still claim a refill, free gems or a Magic Find boost if you want them. Nothing is shown unless you tap to watch it.

I ran out of stamina

Stamina refills on its own over time, and the bar at the top shows how long until the next point. You never have to pay to keep playing — waiting always works.

A reward ad did not give me my reward

The reward only counts if the ad finishes playing. If you closed it early, try again. If a completed ad still pays nothing, email us and tell us which offer it was.

How do I delete my data?

Deleting the app removes the game from your device, but the keychain copy of your save can survive it. To erase everything, open Settings inside the game and tap Reset Game before deleting the app — that permanently wipes your character, inventory and progress, including the backup copy. It cannot be undone.
