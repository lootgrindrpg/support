Privacy Policy

Lootgrind RPG · Effective 16 September 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Lootgrind RPG app for mobile devices, together with any related services operated by [DEVELOPER NAME] (collectively, the "Application"). [DEVELOPER NAME] is hereby referred to as the "Service Provider".

In plain terms: the Application has no accounts and no sign-in, and the Service Provider operates no servers that hold your game data. Your character and everything on it are stored on your own device. The information described below is collected automatically by third-party software the Application uses for advertising, analytics and crash reporting.

Information Collection and Use

The Application collects information when you download and use it. This information is gathered automatically by the third-party services listed further below, and may include:

  • Your device's Internet Protocol (IP) address, and the approximate region derived from it
  • Your device model, operating system and version, and language
  • The screens of the Application that you visit, the time and date of your visit, and the time spent on those screens
  • The time spent in the Application, and how often you open it
  • Gameplay events such as levels gained, zones entered, tutorial progress, ads watched, and in-game currency spent
  • Purchase events, recorded as the product bought together with its price and currency — never your payment details
  • Advertising identifiers, including the Apple advertising identifier where you have allowed tracking
  • Technical details accompanying a crash or error, such as the app version and device model

The Application does not ask you to create an account, and does not request or collect your name, email address, postal address, phone number or precise location. The only personal information the Service Provider ever receives directly is what you choose to put in an email when you contact support.

What Stays On Your Device

Your save — character, level, gear, inventory, currencies, town buildings and settings — is stored on your device. On iPhone a second copy is kept in the device keychain so your character survives deleting and re-downloading the Application. Both are included in your device's own backup if you have backups enabled, which is handled by Apple.

The Service Provider never receives a copy of your save. That also means a lost character cannot be restored, and in-game currency cannot be added or removed remotely, because there is no account to act on.

Cookies and Tracking Technologies

The Application itself does not use cookies. Its third-party SDKs may use device identifiers, advertising identifiers and similar technologies to support functionality, analytics, advertising and service delivery. Where required by applicable law, the Service Provider obtains consent before non-essential tracking technologies are used: on iPhone the Application presents Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt, and where local law requires an advertising consent choice, Google's consent form is shown and can be reopened at any time from Privacy Options in the in-game Settings.

Third Party Access

The Application uses third-party services that have their own privacy policies covering how they handle data. The Service Provider does not sell your personal information. Below are the third-party services used by the Application, and links to their policies:

  • Google AdMob — serves the advertisements, including the optional reward ads you choose to watch, and may use device and advertising identifiers to measure and personalise them. How Google uses data · Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Analytics for Firebase — records the gameplay and usage events described above. Firebase privacy and security
  • Sentry — receives crash and error reports. This is deliberately configured not to send personal information: IP address, cookies and user context are switched off, so reports carry the error and its technical context rather than who encountered it. Sentry Privacy Policy
  • Apple — processes in-app purchases. Apple handles the payment and the Service Provider never sees your payment details; the Application learns only that a purchase completed so it can grant what you bought. Apple Privacy Policy

The Service Provider may disclose information:

  • as required by law, such as to comply with a subpoena or similar legal process;
  • when they believe in good faith that disclosure is necessary to protect their rights, protect your safety or the safety of others, investigate fraud, or respond to a government request;
  • with the trusted service providers listed above, who work on their behalf and are bound by their own obligations regarding the data they process.

The third-party service providers above may transfer and process personal data in countries outside your country of residence, including outside the European Economic Area. Where applicable law requires safeguards for such transfers, those providers rely on mechanisms including:

  • Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission
  • Adequacy decisions or other legally recognised transfer mechanisms
  • Your consent, where required and legally permitted

Data protection laws in other countries may differ from those in your jurisdiction. Where required by law, appropriate safeguards are applied and any consent required for the transfer is obtained.

Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal data relating to you, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to data portability. You may also withdraw consent at any time where processing is based on consent, without affecting processing carried out before withdrawal.

To exercise these rights, contact the Service Provider at lootgrindrpg@gmail.com. Because the Service Provider holds no accounts and no copy of your data, most requests concern data held by the third parties above; the Service Provider will respond and direct you to the relevant provider where the data sits with them. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Your California Privacy Rights (CCPA/CPRA)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to know what personal information is collected, the right to delete personal information, the right to correct inaccurate personal information, the right to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information, and the right not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights.

The Service Provider does not sell personal information for money. Where you have allowed tracking, the use of advertising identifiers for personalised advertising may be considered "sharing" for cross-context behavioural advertising under the CPRA. You can opt out at any time by declining or turning off tracking in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking, and through Privacy Options in the in-game Settings where that choice is offered. To exercise your CCPA/CPRA rights, contact lootgrindrpg@gmail.com.

Opt-Out Rights

You can limit or stop collection at any time:

  • Decline the tracking prompt, or turn tracking off later in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. Ads still appear, but are not personalised using the advertising identifier.
  • Use Privacy Options in the in-game Settings, where local law requires that choice, to change your advertising consent.
  • Buy Remove Ads to stop the interruptive ads. The optional reward ads remain available if you want them.
  • Uninstall the Application. This stops any further collection from your device, but does not by itself delete information already transmitted to the third parties above.

Data Retention

The Service Provider operates no servers and therefore retains no game data of its own. Email you send to support is kept only as long as needed to deal with your request.

  • Data on your device: kept until you delete it. Open the in-game Settings and tap Reset Game to permanently erase your character, inventory and progress, including the keychain backup copy. Deleting the app removes the game itself, but the keychain copy can survive a delete, so use Reset Game first if you want everything gone.
  • Data held by third parties: retained according to their own retention policies and settings, as described in the policies linked above.
  • Aggregated and anonymised data: may be retained indefinitely, as it no longer identifies you.
  • Data required for legal compliance: retained as long as applicable law requires.
